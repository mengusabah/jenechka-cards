Jenechka Cards vocabulary data

The application's MIT license covers application code, not the third-party
linguistic materials in this directory. Sources are identified per record in
v1/*.json and fully attributed in v1/catalog.json and source-data/*.json.
The app retains dataset attribution in imported decks and exported backups.

  Source ID: wiktionary-kaikki-expanded.

  Content: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International.
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
  https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Wiktionary:Copyrights
  https://kaikki.org/dictionary/English/index.html
  Changes: select explicit English entry/POS/etymology/translation-table
  meanings, including nested parent-sense tables; filter forms and register;
  retain source annotations, variants and sense evidence; select equivalent
  forms; use source translation headings as English sense labels; convert to
  JSON. Explicit source topic associations are retained when available.
  These adaptations remain under CC BY-SA 4.0. Extraction provenance, source
  entry links and input hashes appear in source-data/expanded-lexicon.json.
  Additional category/context assignments are individually reviewed exact
  dictionary senses, recorded in source-data/topic-supplement.json and
  source-data/context-supplement.json. This is model-assisted semantic curation
  by the coding assistant, not independent human or native-speaker review.
  A raw parent-sense topic is used automatically only when its gloss matches
  the translation heading; unrelated inherited topics are not propagated.
  Targeted form corrections and exclusions are recorded in
  source-data/form-review.json. Replacements are literal alternatives in the
  same source table; uncertain or mismatched forms are omitted.

The third-party materials retain the respective terms above. ShareAlike
material is not relicensed as MIT. Source metadata and attribution should
accompany redistributed adaptations, including vocabulary backups.
Dictionary English glosses do not establish independent English CEFR levels
or cross-dictionary sense equivalence. Source frequency ranks, semantic
categories and CEFR classifications are distinct kinds of evidence.

No source author endorses this application. Source classifications and
dictionary-derived translations are not a claim of official certification or
independent native-speaker review of every resulting card. User edits may
change imported copies; attribution remains attached to their original data.

Recorded quality review and editorial material, 2026-10-20
  All thirteen languages underwent structural, script, register and POS
  screening. Individually accepted repairs/exclusions are in quality-west.json,
  quality-romance-ru.json and quality-asian.json. Exact source snapshots remain
  unchanged. Source-attested alternatives, annotation cleanup and original
  editorial repairs are distinguished. This is model-assisted review, not a
  guarantee that every translation has independent native-speaker approval.
  Parenthetical explanations/readings are removed from study faces; original
  annotations and sense evidence remain in source-data.
  Frequency preferences pin the target's ordinary reviewed sense before
  translation lookup. Unsupported grammatical tokens and homograph-only
  matches are omitted, never replaced by a different sense or a lower rank.

  Original Jenechka vocabulary and level-estimation code: MIT.
  Source IDs: jenechka-editorial; jenechka-estimates.
  https://github.com/mengusabah/jenechka-cards/blob/main/LICENSE
  New original editorial concepts are in source-data/editorial.json.
  Dataset-derived corrections remain adaptations under their original source
  licences; adding editorial provenance does not relicense ShareAlike data.
  Additional exact-sense topic assignments are in
  source-data/topic-expansion-reviewed.json.

  Bidirectional pair review covers Turkish with English, German, Spanish
  and Russian. Other prebuilt language combinations are deferred. Each
  pair's explicitly inspected sense IDs and proposed repairs are retained in
  source-data/pair-reviews; shared-form disagreements are adjudicated in
  pair-review-resolutions.json. The build applies pair-corrections.json,
  preserving original source attribution alongside editorial provenance.
  This is model-assisted semantic review, not independent human translator
  certification. Replacement words receive their own native corpus ranks
  and level classifications; old headword ranks are not transferred.

  Source CEFR assignments remain separate from editorial estimates. All
  estimates are visibly marked in the app and in copied-deck notices.
  The method combines native familiarity, native editorial anchors, exact
  meaning/domain, POS, register and language-specific word/phrase complexity;
  source-data/cefr-reviewed-*.json records individual native-sense overrides.
  No English CEFR label is transferred to another language, no native corpus
  rank alone defines a level, and HSK/JLPT/TOPIK levels are not converted.
  Reference framework: https://www.coe.int/en/web/common-european-framework-reference-languages/cefr-descriptors
